# HANDOFF — 2026-09-27

Four categories, kept apart on purpose. Software that is deployed is not
capability that is demonstrated; capability demonstrated against substituted
transport is not market evidence; and none of the three is an owner decision.
Collapsing any two of them is how a demonstration gets read as performance.

**Status of this document.** The release readback found a real production defect
and it is written up below as the first thing in section 1, because a handoff
that leads with what worked and buries what did not is a sales document. The
corrected build is gated and being deployed; the lines marked **PENDING** are
the ones this document cannot honestly fill until that readback returns.

## 1 · DEPLOYED SOFTWARE

### 1.1 · What the readback found, and what it means

`07c67f3` deployed cleanly through the API-only route and served. Asked for the
funded command-centre section, the running process answered:

```
section  Funded book
error    UndefinedColumnError: column "residual_qty" does not exist
```

The boot log says why. At 03:22:51 migration 126 raised

```
function bettor_funded_order_is_outstanding(text) does not exist
CONTEXT: SQL function "bettor_funded_position_is_open" during inlining
```

the whole file rolled back, and `start.sh` logs `migrate failed — serving
anyway`. So the build went live with the *previous* funded schema.

**The cause was the server, not the file.** The migration applies cleanly on a
fresh database, applies cleanly against a database carrying production's exact
pre-state, and has had exactly one version in its history. Production runs
**PostgreSQL 18.6**; every database this file had ever met — local, and every
gate checkout — is **16.13**. PostgreSQL 17 and later run maintenance work, an
index build included, under a deliberately safe `search_path` of
`pg_catalog, pg_temp`, and a SQL function body is re-parsed when the planner
inlines it. The two partial indexes in 126 have a predicate whose body called
two other funded functions *unqualified*, so at build time those names could not
be resolved. Reproduced on 16 by setting that path explicitly: the unqualified
body raises the production error verbatim, including the `during inlining`
context; the qualified body builds.

Fixed at the cause in `714680b`: every funded SQL function body now names funded
functions and tables with their schema. Two new tests fail against the old text
and pass against the new one — one evaluates each funded predicate with
`search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp` set explicitly, which is the *condition*
rather than the version, and one reads every funded body back out of `pg_proc`
so the function somebody adds next is covered too.

Three things are worth saying plainly about this:

- **The gate could not have caught it, and that is a fact about the gate.** A
  suite that runs against PostgreSQL 16 cannot see a PostgreSQL 17+ behaviour.
  The instrument's scope is now recorded, and the two tests move the check into
  a form 16 *can* evaluate.
- **The panel behaved correctly.** It reported the failure instead of showing an
  empty book. A funded section that rendered "0 positions, $0.00" over an
  unmigrated schema would have been the serious outcome; this was the designed
  one.
- **Nothing was at risk.** The funded tables hold zero rows, no funded order has
  ever been sent, and every submission switch was off throughout.

### 1.3 · What the funded lane enforces, in code

- Order terminality and inventory closure are separate predicates. A FILLED
  entry keeps its holding in every rail — per-event, per-market, correlated —
  until an evidenced exit or an authoritative settlement removes it. One open
  *position*, not one outstanding order.
- Exits reserve their inventory under `FOR UPDATE` on the position, so two
  concurrent exits and a retry after an ambiguous answer cannot oversell. An
  oversell that reaches the book becomes a discrepancy row, not a clamped zero.
- Recovery never adopts an order it cannot prove is ours. This venue's
  `CreateOrderParams` accepts no client order identifier, so a lost
  acknowledgement stays UNRESOLVED with its exposure preserved and the term
  match recorded for a human.
- A fill, its economic events and the residual/closure transition are one
  commit. An interruption at any boundary rolls back whole, and a redelivery
  repairs missing events and books a late commission in the same transaction.
- Realised P&L and drawdown are sums over a ledger. Unrealised P&L is reported
  UNMEASURED by name, never zero.
- Servicing outlives entry permission: an exit is gated on ownership of the
  position's own row, not on the grant to add exposure. Reconciliation and
  settlement reads are never gated at all.
- Exit selection runs through the deployed decision path and relaxes none of
  its evidence rules: the venue's executable book under the entry lane's own
  freshness policy, EV_HOLD under its freshness and payout-event rules, an
  ESTABLISHED settlement attestation, and the ranking's MIN_IMPROVEMENT gate.
- Proceeds and the venue's wire price are separate numbers. The submission
  refuses a wire that would receive less per contract than the level the action
  was selected on.
- A future venue timestamp gets no fresh certificate; skew is a bounded stated
  allowance against our receipt instant. Request, receipt and decision are
  separate, the age is taken at the decision instant, and a delayed submission
  cannot reuse an expired assessment.

## 3 · REMAINING MARKET EVIDENCE

None of this is closed by writing more code.

- **Source calibration.** The de-vigged Pinnacle probability is the *source's*
  number. Its calibration against outcomes is measured by
  `external_source_calibration` and is NOT established. Owner-approved entries
  under the research waiver are labelled `UNCALIBRATED_RESEARCH_SHADOW` for
  exactly this reason. An approval does not calibrate a source.
- **Settlement compatibility, per fixture.** A funded exit requires an
  ESTABLISHED attestation. In production most positions will be restricted
  until the venue's prose is read and compared — the same restriction the entry
  lane already applies. This is a market-evidence dependency.
- **Venue book freshness basis.** What `marketData.transactTime` denotes is not
  established by the venue's published contract. The lane's policy is stated
  rather than derived: an absent or unparseable clock is UNMEASURED and refuses.
  That is a decision with a reason, not a mathematical claim.
- **Candidate qualification.** `NO_TRADE` on a cycle is a result, not a gap. The
  562-market cycle above is the instrument working; what it does not yet show is
  a qualifying candidate.
- **The shadow lane's `MAX_EVENT_EXPOSURE` blindness.** `OPEN_BOOK_SQL` selects
  no event key. Unchanged and still recorded. The funded lane carries a
  canonical event key on every intent and does not depend on it.

## WHAT I WOULD NOT CLAIM FROM THIS WORK

- That the funded lane has traded. It has not.
- That +$1.70 is performance. It is a provisional figure from a development
  demonstration against substituted transport.
- That the lifecycle is proven against a live venue. Every venue interaction in
  this session was a substituted transport at one seam.
- That a green gate means a working release. The gate on `07c67f3` was green by
  identity and the funded schema still did not apply, because the gate's
  database is two major versions behind production's.
- That the gate's 177 failures are acceptable in themselves. They are the
  *baseline's* failures, reproduced exactly; the claim is that this work adds
  none, not that the suite is clean.
