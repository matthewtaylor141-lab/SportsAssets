The week the book went quiet

Six measured days settled −$58.18 on 58 markets. Two weeks ago it was 966.

Venue truth for Mon Sep 14 – Sat Sep 19 is essentially flat: −$58.18 realized on $129,478.82 of settled cost, 46 wins to 12 losses. The striking number is not the P&L, it is the volume. Week 36 settled 966 markets; this week settled 58 — 6% of the activity on 71% of the cost, which is the signature of a book that has stopped taking small positions and is running down a handful of large ones. The copy sleeves settled nothing at all.

THIS EDITION COVERS SIX DAYS, NOT SEVEN. The venue-truth table holds no row for Sunday Sep 20. Its most recent write was 2026-09-21 01:57 UTC, before the Sunday ET day had closed, and nothing has run since — 8.4 hours stale at the time of this report. Every figure here is therefore a six-day figure and is labelled as such. Sunday is absent, not zero.

THE USUAL DETAIL SECTIONS ARE NOT IN THIS EDITION. The by-sport split, position-size buckets, largest wins and losses, per-whale sleeve detail and the whale-upstream curves are all folded from engine-diagnostic export blobs. Those logs download from results-receiver.actions.githubusercontent.com, which this environment's egress policy denies — the same unreachable host the Week 36 edition flagged. Rather than estimate them, this edition reports only what the database can be made to prove.

Operational and safety notes

Material to management, found while this report was being prepared. These are engineering findings, not P&L, and they are reported here because they bear on what the account is capable of doing unattended.

  • An order route is enabled by default and does not read the master switch. workers/whale_exits.py → live_executor.mirror_exit is registered in the running workers service; its env flag WHALE_EXIT_ENABLED is absent from that service and defaults to on; its whale allowlist is populated; PMUS credentials are present; the pause row live_trading_paused is absent, and the code treats an absent row as not paused. mirror_exit never calls active_venue(), so LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED does not gate it. One gate remains unread — LIVE_COPY_HALT, set to a single character whose value is not visible from here. It has not fired: filled orders stood at 52 at 01:57Z and at 52 again at 10:11Z.
  • There is no global execution gate. Six order-capable routes were inventoried; they consult three disjoint stop mechanisms and no single switch reaches all of them. Recommended remedy is one authoritative fail-closed check above every order function, defaulting to refuse.
  • A second engine exists and is configured live. The edge-shadow service holds its own venue credentials and reads mode: LIVE_BETA. It is currently suspended and has produced no logs, so there is no exposure today; it would resume in that configuration.
  • Standing constraints hold. mirror_live=false, COMMAND undeployed, and the BETTOR research lane has no order path in its import graph — real-order activity NONE, real capital at risk 0 for that lane. That statement is scoped to BETTOR and is not a claim about the whole system.
