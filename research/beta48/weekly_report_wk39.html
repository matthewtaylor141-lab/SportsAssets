What the two missing days mean

History runs 2026-08-06 to 2026-09-27: 51 of 53 days present, 2 missing, and the two missing days are the only gaps in the whole span. Both neighbours are present.

What absence means, from the writer rather than from the crawl clock. A row exists only for a day that HAD settlements. `persistable_day_rows` builds its tuples from `daily_series(rows)`, so a day with no settlement rows produces no tuple and therefore no row. Absence is therefore 'no settlements', NOT 'not looked at' -- provided the day fell inside a written range.

A pagination guard does exist. `pm_persist_bounds` returns the ET days the crawl PROVABLY holds in full as a (first_day, before_day) pair, 'or None when no day is provable and the ledger must not be written', and a capped crawl's partial edge day is excluded. So the writer already refuses to record days it cannot prove it covered.

And here is what I could not close. The crawl does NOT persist its provable range. Section 7 looked for any crawl cursor, page or coverage row and found only `bettor_live_probe_state` and three `rn1x_*` cursors -- nothing for venue truth. So `pm_persist_bounds`'s answer is computed per run and discarded, and WHETHER A GIVEN PAST DAY WAS INSIDE A WRITTEN RANGE CANNOT BE VERIFIED AFTER THE FACT.

Sep 21-22 are therefore UNRESOLVED, not explained. The writer's semantics make 'no settlements' the most likely reading, and it is consistent with the account opening no orders after Sep 11 and holding fewer positions to resolve. But a pair of days that no crawl provably covered would look identical, and nothing on file distinguishes them.

What would close it: persist pm_persist_bounds' (first_day, before_day) per crawl run. Then a missing day inside a recorded range is 'no settlements' and one outside it is 'never covered', and the question stops recurring every week.

A discriminator that did not work. I intended to settle it with a second, independent source: orders WE settled on a missing day. Section 3 returned 0 orders for both days -- but live_orders is silent for the ENTIRE week (no lane wrote after Sep 11), so it returns 0 for Sep 23-27 as well, which DO have venue-truth rows. The discriminator is vacuous over this window and I am reporting it as such rather than quoting its answer.
