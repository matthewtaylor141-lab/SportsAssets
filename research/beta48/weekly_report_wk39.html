Operational and safety notes

Material to management. Engineering findings, not P&L, reported because they bear on what the account can do unattended.

  • The global execution gate Week 38 recommended now exists and is deployed. execution_gate is invoked inside pmus.submit_fok and pmus.close_position rather than at the call sites, so it covers callers that pass the function as a callable — which no call-site check caught. Its denial raises rather than returning, and an unreadable control row fails closed. Week 38's finding that "no single switch reaches all of them" has been acted on.
  • The kill switch is engaged. live_trading_paused reads true in the database this week. Week 38 reported that row absent, with the code treating absent as not paused. That specific exposure is closed for the routes behind the gate.
  • Funded submission ships disabled and no funded order has ever been sent. FUNDED_EXIT_SUBMISSION_ENABLED is False in the shipped code. The funded book holds 0 intents and 0 residual contracts.
  • The scheduled engine is running and qualifying nothing. Its last cycle completed 2026-09-28 at 03:36:51 UTC on the deployed build, state LIVE, label ZERO_EVALUATED__INPUT_PATH_BLOCKED. Measured cause, this week: the execution estimate is produced on 5.2% of evaluations, and the largest single reason is NO_OBSERVED_DEPTH_INSIDE_THE_BREAK_EVEN_LIMIT on 488 rows — the venue offering no affordable depth, which is an economic finding and not a software fault. The largest actionable cause is an absent independent fair value on 399 rows.
  • Venue mechanics established from the venue's own documentation. Polymarket US runs one instrument per market (the YES side) on one central limit order book: buying NO at a is selling YES at 1−a. Its two published collateral-return mechanisms are portfolio-margin optimisations, stated by the venue as "not a reduction in actual risk", and neither releases capital on a completed pair. This narrows the pairing programme rather than widening it.
