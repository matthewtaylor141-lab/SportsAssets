# 1 — Golden Market Validation Pack

Purpose: freeze mapping and economic-equivalence truth before the upgraded
Kalshi/Polymarket system is judged.

## Evidence classes

`CAPTURED_VENUE_FIXTURE`
means the case is grounded in a venue fixture already captured in the
SportsAssets repository.

`FROZEN_INTEGRATION_CASE`
means the expected semantic behavior is frozen now, but the exact Kalshi
production payload has not yet been supplied by the venue rep. These cases must
be replaced or supplemented—not silently re-labeled—when real payloads arrive.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Titles are not money-path evidence.
- Competition / league / event / period / line / subject must map structurally.
- Economic aliases are equal only when full payoff vectors are equal.
- Unknown settlement state refuses.
- Three-way NO contracts are not assumed to equal the opposing YES.
- Best route is lowest *all-in executable* cost, not display price.
- Stale routes cannot win.
- Same-venue and cross-venue arbitrage are both valid topologies when the
  payoff floor is proven positive after costs.
