## Simplifications versus the spec

1. **World-only entries.** A refused decision that a world would take is filled at its planned qty, capped by the recorded depth within limit. It fills at the planned acquisition price and fee rate and is held to settlement. No fill-probability or impact model is applied, and no Xavier management is invented. A recorded ENTER that did not fill does not fill in the world either.
2. **Exits.** Immediate and Karen exits sell into the latest recorded book at or before the exit instant (max age 120 s) at the position's entry fee rate. The real fee schedule is used only when Xavier's thesis froze an IMMEDIATE_EXIT plan. Paper hedge legs are dropped in exit worlds.
3. **Sizing** scales the recorded P&L linearly. There is no depth-walk impact model.
4. **Allocator worlds** scale each position's recorded return per dollar by the world's notional. "Equal weight" means ten equal $100 slots of the $1,000 sleeve, capped by the free sleeve. "Independent sizing" means 5% of the sleeve per position with no portfolio cap. Correlation reduction is measured as the reduction in maximum single-fixture capital share, not as a return correlation.
5. **Eddie, Scout and the tournament** are interfaces only. Their worlds, metrics and criterion are UNAVAILABLE until 217 and 218 provide the views. The Eddie world assumes Eddie's VWAP and fee for the recorded quantity.
