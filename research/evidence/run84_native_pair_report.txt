13. CAPACITY
   Not estimated, and deliberately not estimated. Capacity is the
   question 'how much can be deployed into an edge'; sections 4, 5, 9
   and 11 find no edge to deploy into. Producing a capacity table for a
   negative-expectancy rule would dress a loss in units of scale.
   The $1,000 / $5,000 / $10,000 / $25,000 / $50,000 ladder is therefore
   reported as NOT_APPLICABLE rather than filled in.
   One capacity fact IS measured and is reported for its own sake: at the
   1 s window, pairs that could source a $1,000 clip on BOTH legs from the
   displayed ladder numbered 2847 of 3186. Depth is not what is missing here.
