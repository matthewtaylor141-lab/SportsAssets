  nfl holds 4 of 5 selected markets (80%).

  S4 caps any one sport at ceil(cap/2) = 4, and it BOUND: NFL took
  exactly 4. But the cap is computed against the CAP (8), not against
  the size the cohort actually reached (5). The frame held only 15
  eligible events across 2 sports, so the cohort under-filled and a
  cap meant to mean 'at most half' delivered 80%.

  So the frozen gate PASSES and the anti-domination INTENT does not.
  I am reporting both rather than letting the passing gate stand for
  the intent. The fix is one line -- cap against len(picks) as it
  grows, not against the target -- and it is NOT applied to this
  sealed run.

  The walk also stopped the instant the gate first passed, at page 6
  of a 40-page bound. Stop-on-first-pass returns the MINIMUM passing
  cohort by construction. Walking further would very likely have
  found more sports and more spread regimes.
