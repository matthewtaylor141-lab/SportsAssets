  WHY GATE 3 FAILED, AND IT IS MY DEFECT AGAIN IN THE SAME SHAPE.

  The frame this run built WAS diverse: sport buckets nfl, soccer
  and other; tick sizes 0.005 and 0.01; price bands P_LOW, P_MID
  and P_HIGH. The walk stopped the moment the FRAME satisfied the
  criterion -- and the criterion was written against the frame.

  But the frame is not the deliverable; the COHORT is. Selection
  then round-robined over (sport bucket, state) across only 16
  game events, most of them NFL, and produced 7 NFL plus 1 boxing,
  every one P_MID and every one PREGAME.

  Phase 2E stopped on 'events and sports' when the cohort needed
  regimes. Phase 2F stopped on frame composition when the cohort
  still needed regimes. Same error, one level further in: a stop
  rule must be written against the thing that has to be diverse,
  and the thing that has to be diverse is the SELECTED COHORT.

  The fix is small and specific: keep walking until a TRIAL
  SELECTION run over the current frame clears the diversity bar,
  not until the frame does. That is a one-function change to the
  stop condition and it is not applied to this sealed run.

  Two named unavailabilities are NOT defects and must not be
  papered over by a rule change: no CFB or MLB game-level event
  and no LIVE event existed in the frame at 15:29Z on a Sunday
  morning UTC. Section 3 says report unavailable categories
  rather than force them, so they are reported.

=== 9. FROZEN MULTI-DAY CAPTURE DESIGN (for approval; NOT started) ===

SCOPE. Observational only. GET on the public gateway, no credential,
no websocket, no order path, mirror_live stays false.

RATE. 0.4 rps nominal, 2.5 s spacing floor, Retry-After honoured in
full, backoff x2 on 429 to a 30 s ceiling, recovery x0.9 per success
only. Pacer drained before every timed plan. No rate discovery.

COHORT. Re-selected at the start of each capture day by the frozen
2F rule: event-level frame over offset pagination, round-robin across
(sport bucket, normalized state) strata, one market per native event,
ordered by native event id. A null primaryTag is not a sport.
Target 8 markets; re-selection is by rule, never by hand.

SAMPLING. Horizon-specific, not one uniform round robin. Per subject,
an observation SET is t0 then t0+5, +10, +30, +60 s, placed exactly
and checked against the spacing floor and the mean ceiling BEFORE
running; a plan that breaches either is not trimmed, it is refused.
Sets repeat on a rotation so each market contributes to the horizons
its schedule actually reached. A market may contribute to some
horizons and not others. NOTHING IS EVER INTERPOLATED.

PER OBSERVATION, RECORD:
     * local request and response wall clock, and monotonic
     * http status, response bytes, response sha256
     * venue transactTime AND stats.lastPriceSample.ts
     * full normalized ladder plus its sha256
     * best bid, best ask, displayed spread
     * touch quantity and notional, both sides
     * cumulative quantity and notional within 0/1/2/5 ticks
     * the market's own tick size, on every row
     * raw period, normalized state, and the justification
     * scheduled game start, event start, endDate -- kept separate
     * a paired /bbo read for the freshness cross-check
     * market state and event id from the frame

DERIVABLE LATER, AND ONLY LATER:
  displayed passive spread; a hypothetical resting quote at the touch
  or one tick behind; whether the market traded through that price;
  near-touch queue state ahead of it; one-leg versus both-leg
  opportunity; 5/10/30/60 s adverse movement; incomplete-pair
  exposure; residual settlement outcome where the venue publishes it.

THE LINE THAT MUST NOT BE CROSSED:
  PASSIVE_TOUCH_PROXY       = the market reached our hypothetical price
  PASSIVE_FILL_PROBABILITY  = NOT_IDENTIFIED
  Touching a price does not prove an order there would have filled.
  Queue position, the size ahead, and whether the print consumed our
  level are all unobserved from public data. The capture measures the
  proxy and names it a proxy, everywhere, permanently.

PROFITABILITY REGISTER. The dataset exists to estimate
  GROSS_SPREAD_CAPTURE - ADVERSE_SELECTION - INCOMPLETE_PAIR_RESIDUAL
  - FEES
Displayed spread is NOT profit. Until the fee formula is resolved the
only two figures that may be computed are:
  PRE_FEE_EXPECTANCY      the first three terms, fees excluded
  BREAK_EVEN_TOTAL_FEE    the total fee per share that would take
                          PRE_FEE_EXPECTANCY to zero
No borrowed fee formula. The 0.06 coefficient is a FIELD, not a rule.

INTEGRITY. Every day's output sealed with per-file sha256 written
after the report is closed, couriered into the repo unmodified, and
re-verified by the analyst in its own process before use.

STOP CONDITIONS. Halt and report on: any 429 rate above 1%% of
requests; any cross-route touch disagreement above 1%%; any identity
mismatch or body collision; any venue state change that invalidates
the cohort. Do not adapt silently.

  THE DESIGN ABOVE IS A DRAFT, NOT THE FROZEN PLAN. It is shown
  because every part of it except cohort selection is settled by
  this run's evidence, and withholding it would hide work that is
  already done. It becomes the frozen plan only once gate 3
