=== 2. BOOK ACTIVITY: OBSERVED_CALIBRATION_ACTIVITY ===
FEATURE_NAME                   = OBSERVED_CALIBRATION_ACTIVITY
SOURCE_STAGE                   = census_book (the preregistered repeated-sampling pass)
MARKETS_SAMPLED                = 60
OBSERVATIONS_PER_MARKET        = [3]
REVISIT_INTERVAL               = 120.0 s (min 120.0, max 120.0)
PER_MARKET_WINDOW_SPAN         = 240.0 s (min 240.0, max 240.0)
STAGE_WALL_CLOCK               = 2026-09-13T03:36:25.473332+00:00 .. 2026-09-13T03:42:23.494944+00:00

  So ACTIVE / QUIET means: did this book's content change across THREE
  observations 120 s apart, spanning 240 s. Nothing more.

  IT IS NOT AN ADMISSION-TIME FEATURE. It cannot be read from a single
  discovery snapshot, and a selection rule that needs it must pay for a
  repeated-sampling pass first, as this run did.

  It is also COARSE, and in a direction that matters. At a 120 s
  interval a book that changed many times between samples records the
  same 'changed' as one that moved once, and a book that moved and
  reverted records QUIET. OBSERVED_CALIBRATION_ACTIVITY is a lower
  bound on change, not a rate. It must not be reported as a
  book-change RATE, and BOOK_ACTIVITY_DIVERSITY = SUFFICIENT means
  only that both coarse labels appear in the cohort.

  Nothing was altered to produce this. The property is STRUCTURAL: in
  the frozen rule, `cap` appears only as an early return inside take().
  The candidate ORDER -- S5's three asymmetry bins in fixed order, then
  regime groups by (-size, key) with members by slug -- never consults
  cap. So the cohort at cap=k is exactly the first k of one sequence,
  and a smaller cap truncates it rather than diverting to a different
  market. FAST is therefore a strict PREFIX of SURVEY, by construction.

  Verified two ways: B[:2] == A on this pool, and on 800 synthetic
  pools (4 sizes x 200 seeds) the subset property held every time.

  THE FAST TIER WAS NEVER RUN. The validation sample exercised the

  measurement, and nothing in this archive demonstrates it.

  Eligibility on the NOMINAL 12.0 s grid, with no interpolation:
     5S_ELIGIBLE_COHORT = NO   12.0 s grid has no sample at t+5s
    10S_ELIGIBLE_COHORT = NO   12.0 s grid has no sample at t+10s
    30S_ELIGIBLE_COHORT = NO   12.0 s grid has no sample at t+30s
    60S_ELIGIBLE_COHORT = YES  5 x 12.0 s

  Jitter is sub-millisecond here, so a t+60 s markout lands on a real
  observation rather than near one. A future capture must re-check
  that: jitter comparable to the horizon would reintroduce exactly the
  interpolation this section exists to refuse.

  The runner's cadence table said 12.0 s 'supports: 30, 60s'. That is
  WRONG for 30 s and is corrected here: 30 is not a whole multiple of
  12, so a 30 s markout off a 12 s grid requires interpolating a state
  never observed. Only 60 s survives (5 x 12.0 s).

  Generalised. Revisit = 2.0 x N, so horizon h is observable only when
  h / (2.0 N) is a whole number:
     N   revisit     5s   10s   30s   60s
     1     2.0 s      -   YES   YES   YES
     2     4.0 s      -     -     -   YES
     3     6.0 s      -     -   YES   YES
     4     8.0 s      -     -     -     -
     5    10.0 s      -   YES   YES   YES
     6    12.0 s      -     -     -   YES
     7    14.0 s      -     -     -     -
     8    16.0 s      -     -     -     -
     9    18.0 s      -     -     -     -
    10    20.0 s      -     -     -   YES

  5 S IS UNREACHABLE AT ANY COHORT SIZE. Every achievable revisit is a
  multiple of 2.0 s and 5 is odd, so h/(2.0 N) is never an integer.
  5S_ELIGIBLE_COHORT = NOT_FEASIBLE at the 0.5 rps ceiling. It is not a
  cohort-size problem and no selection fixes it; it would take a
  different base spacing, which needs an RPS change, which is locked
  (RPS_LIMIT_NOT_ESTABLISHED). Per the standing instruction, an
  unobserved 5 s state is NOT interpolated -- so it is not reported.

  A 'first observation at or after t+5s' statistic is measurable, but
  it is a DIFFERENT ESTIMAND with its own bias and would need its own
  preregistration. It is not the 5 s markout and is not substituted.

  N=5 (10.0 s) is the only size reaching 10, 30 AND 60. The chosen
  N=6 (12.0 s) reaches 60 alone, and N=2 (4.0 s) also reaches 60 alone.

  and the census spent its event diversity on an alphabetical cut.

  be sized N=1 (2.0 s: reaches 10, 30, 60) and N=5 (10.0 s: reaches 10,
  30, 60), not 2 and 6 -- which between them reach only 60. That is a
  FUTURE design note. Phase 2C's selection is untouched.
