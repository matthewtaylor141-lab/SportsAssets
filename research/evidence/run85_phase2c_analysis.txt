=== AUDIT 1. TIME TO EVENT IS NOT MEASURED ===

of those, NEGATIVE (event passed)  : 19
range (days)                       : -101.24 to -93.49
bins assigned                      : {'T_LT_24H': 19}

  Every value is negative, so the event each startTime names has
  already happened. These are season-long futures: startTime is the
  season's start, not the resolution of the contract. The binning
  tested `tte < 86400` with no lower bound, so every negative value
  landed in T_LT_24H.

  TIME_TO_EVENT_DIVERSITY = INSUFFICIENT is RETRACTED.
  The venue supplied a field; the field does not measure the thing.
  The dimension was NOT MEASURED, which is a different answer from
  measured-and-narrow, and it must not be reported as the latter.
  TIME_TO_EVENT_DIVERSITY = NOT_IDENTIFIED

=== AUDIT 2. NO GAME-LEVEL MARKET WAS REACHED ===
  MARKET_STATUS_OPEN       futures     138
  MARKET_STATUS_RESOLVED   futures      10
  MARKET_STATUS_OPEN       election      2

  sportsMarketType is `futures` or `election` on every one of the
  150 detail probes. A season-long futures book (worst record, batting
  average leader, division winner) is not the market BETTOR mirrors:
  RN1's flow is game-level -- moneylines, spreads, totals. A cohort
  drawn from this pool would characterize passive fills in a market
  structure we do not trade.

  This is not a binning problem and more bins cannot fix it. It is a
  DISCOVERY problem: /v1/events?active=true&closed=false returned a
  first page of season-long events at 03:40Z. Whether game markets
  are absent at that hour, are on a later page, or need a different
  query is NOT ESTABLISHED by this run.

  GAME_LEVEL_MARKET_COVERAGE = NONE_OBSERVED

  tick size by market type (per-market storage was the right call):
    futures    tick=0.01      138
    futures    tick=0.001      10
    election   tick=0.001       2

  Tick is NOT constant across the venue. Phase 2B observed 0.001;
  138 of 150 markets here are 0.01. PMUS_ORDER_PRICE_MIN_TICK_SIZE
  is a PER-MARKET field and no single value may be carried forward.
  A spread measured in ticks is comparable only within one tick size.

=== AUDIT 5. THE ASYMMETRY MEASURE IS CONTAMINATED ===
two-sided-or-one-sided books sampled in the census : 60
books carrying an offer level at px >= 0.95          : 60 of 60
median share of ASK notional sitting at px >= 0.95  : 84.2%
range                                              : 58.2% .. 100.0%
most repeated far-ask level                        : 27499.0000 shares @ 0.9900, in 53 books

  asym_bin was log10(WHOLE displayed ask notional / WHOLE displayed
  bid notional). Every book in the census carries a large resting
  block near the top of the price range, and the same block appears
  at the bottom of the bid side. The two are comparable in SHARES and
  wildly different in DOLLARS, because notional is price x quantity:
  27,500 shares at 0.99 is $27,225; the same 27,500 shares at 0.01 is
  $275. A 99x dollar gap out of a symmetric parking order.

  So the whole-ladder measure mostly reports 'this book has a far-end
  block', which is true of every book, and it reports it as ask
  heaviness. It is not measuring side competition near the touch --
  which is the thing section 9 says realizable passive fills depend on.

  Re-derived with the far ends (px>=0.95, px<=0.05) excluded:
    books with inside-range depth on BOTH sides      : 7
    corrected classification                         : {'BALANCED': 4, 'ASK_HEAVY': 2, 'BID_HEAVY': 1}

  BID-HEAVY BOOKS DO EXIST. The earlier reading that none exists was
  produced by the contaminated measure, not by the venue.

  The SAME test applied to the two Phase 2B books, because section 9
  is founded on their 27-30x figure:
    tec-mlb-champ-2026-09-27-lad           whole-ladder   27.5x   inside-range   1.59x  -> BALANCED
    tec-mlb-nlchamp-2026-09-27-lad         whole-ladder   29.9x   inside-range   0.30x  -> BID_HEAVY

  PHASE2B_DEPTH_ASYMMETRY_27_30X = RETRACTED AS A QUEUE-COMPETITION
  READING. The number is arithmetically correct over the whole
  displayed ladder and it is the wrong ladder for the question. Near
  the money one of those two books is close to balanced and the other
  is BID-heavy -- the opposite sign from the one carried forward.

  DEPTH_ASYMMETRY_DIVERSITY = NOT_IDENTIFIED
  Not INSUFFICIENT: the dimension was measured with an invalid
  instrument, so the cohort's coverage of it is unknown rather than
  narrow. S5 forced asymmetry coverage over the contaminated bins, so
  the market the rule selected as BALANCED was selected for a reason
  that does not survive this audit. The rule's MECHANISM is sound and
  tested; the FEATURE it was fed is not.

=== AUDIT 6. HOW A LEG IS ADDRESSED ===
candidates whose long_side == short_side : 19 of 19

  The A7 structure check is real: it requires marketSides to hold
  exactly two entries, one with long=true and one with long=false.
  All 19 passed it. But both entries carry the SAME identifier
  string -- the market slug. The venue distinguishes the legs by the
  BOOLEAN, not by a distinct per-leg token id.

  PMUS_LEG_ADDRESSED_BY = marketSides[].long BOOLEAN
  PMUS_DISTINCT_PER_LEG_IDENTIFIER = NOT_PRESENT

  Nothing is ordered in this run and nothing will be. Recorded
  because any future code that selects a side by identifier string
  would select nothing, and would do so silently.
