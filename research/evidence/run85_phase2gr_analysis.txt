=== B. FROZEN CAPTURE SPECIFICATION ===
COHORT      the 12 markets above, one per native event, re-selected
            each capture day by the frozen 2G-R selector -- never by
            hand. A market whose event ends drops out; the selector
            refills from the same rule.
ACCESS      GET on the public gateway only. No credential, no
            websocket, no order path. mirror_live stays false.
RATE        0.4 rps nominal, 2.5 s spacing floor, Retry-After honoured
            exactly, x2 backoff on 429 to a 30 s ceiling, x0.9 recovery
            per success only. Pacer DRAINED before every timed set.
SAMPLING    horizon sets, not a uniform round robin. One set = reads at
            t0 and t0+5, +10, +30, +60 s for one market. 4 sets run
            concurrently, staggered, so the merged timeline never
            breaches the spacing floor or the mean ceiling. The plan is
            validated before each window and REFUSED, not trimmed, if
            it would breach either.
RECORD      per observation: both local clocks and monotonic; http
            status, bytes, sha256; transactTime AND
            stats.lastPriceSample.ts; full ladder + its sha256; best
            bid/ask and spread; touch qty and notional both sides;
            cumulative qty and notional within 0/1/2/5 ticks; the
            market's own tick on every row; raw period, normalized
            state and its justification; gameStartTime, event
            startTime and endDate kept separate; a paired /bbo read
            for the cross-route freshness check; event id and slug.
SEMANTICS   HORIZON_OBSERVATION_AVAILABLE, BOOK_CHANGED_BY_HORIZON and
            MARKOUT stay three separate fields. An unchanged book at a
            horizon is MARKOUT = 0, never a missing row. The sample is
            NEVER conditioned on the book having moved.
INTEGRITY   each day sealed with per-file sha256 written after the
            report is closed, couriered unmodified, re-verified by the
            analyst in its own process.
STOP        halt and report on: 429s above 1%% of requests; cross-route
            touch disagreement above 1%%; any identity mismatch or body
            collision; a venue change that invalidates the cohort.
            Never adapt silently.

  displayed passive spread, per market and over time
  hypothetical resting quote at the touch or N ticks behind
  PASSIVE_TOUCH_PROXY -- did the market reach that price
  near-touch queue state ahead of that price at 0/1/2/5 ticks
  adverse movement at 5/10/30/60 s, signed against the quoted side
  one-leg versus both-leg opportunity on the same market
  incomplete-pair exposure: one side reached, the other not
  residual settlement outcome where the venue publishes it

                       - incomplete-pair residual
  BREAK_EVEN_TOTAL_FEE = the per-share total fee that takes
                         PRE_FEE_EXPECTANCY to zero

    a FIELD, not a rule, and no borrowed formula may enter a result.
