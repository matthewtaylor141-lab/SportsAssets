# RC6.3b software-reds hourly census (lane H0 readback)

**What this is.** The first-loss census (`coverage_first_loss.census`, COVERAGE_FIRST_LOSS_CENSUS_V1,
taxonomy REFUSAL_TAXONOMY_V1 of this tree) replayed over 24 one-hour windows of the production
ledger, SOFTWARE by code per hour -- the instrument the RC6.3b root-cause audit asked for, so the
84 of the approved-judge packet (pm-acceptance-ec8b892dcf18387601aa668469c10f70c6e05bbc, gate
`software_reds_zero`, read 2026-10-10 09:54:34Z) is one row of a series and every lane that moves
it can be measured on the same instrument.

**The gate's order (the correction over the first readback).** `coverage_first_loss.first_loss_of_event`
takes an event's earliest-stage decision and, between two decisions at that stage, the FIRST IN RECORD
ORDER; the gate's record order is `DECISIONS_SQL`'s `ORDER BY decided_at`. The statement does not select
`decided_at`, and the first export of this series (38069842557) ordered a valuation's decisions by
strategy name instead, so where a valuation carried two same-stage decisions with different codes
(typically `DEREK_ENTRY_POLICY_V2` beside the `PINNACLE_*` wrappers) the replay took the alphabetically
first, not the first decided. The export now carries each decision's `decided_at` (as an epoch) and
`decision_id`; the replay sorts a valuation's decisions by them, refuses an export without them, and names
per row every cell that an EXACT `decided_at` tie between two different codes would leave to the gate's
own read order (`order_ambiguity` in the JSON, a `*` in the table). On this series there is no such tie in
any of the 24 hours: of the 10,161 valuations carrying two same-stage decisions with different codes, the
two first candidates were decided 66 ms to 232 s apart (median 116 ms, p90 456 ms, none under 10 ms), so
every cell below is the gate's reading, not one of several.

Measured against the first readback on the identical rows: the SOFTWARE total, ECONOMIC, EXTERNAL and
UNCLASSIFIED of every row are unchanged (as are `first_loss` by stage and the event counts); the SOFTWARE
per-code cells changed in 14 of the 24 rows (0, 1, 3, 4, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23), by 1 to 3
events per cell, all at FAIR_VALUE. The clearest signature: `NO_QUALIFIED_PINNACLE_PROBABILITY` (Derek's
code, shown as up to 2 per hour in the first table) leaves the table entirely, and `NO_QUALIFIED_MODEL`
(the code the earlier-decided `PINNACLE_*` wrapper carries, unwrapped by the census) enters it, up to 3
per hour in 13 rows; `FEED_QUOTE_OLDER_THAN_LIMIT` at FAIR_VALUE loses one in 9 rows (its maximum, 9 in
row 23, stands); `PINNAPI_PRIMARY_INPUT_CHANGED` is 31 not 32 in row 0 and `PINNAPI_PRIMARY_NO_EXACT_
FIXTURE` at FAIR_VALUE 14 not 13 there and 3 not 1 in row 20. The ECONOMIC split by code (not in this
table; the gate names no ECONOMIC code) moved the same way in 22 rows, between `BELOW_MIN_GROSS_EDGE` and
`GROSS_EDGE_CLEARS_THRESHOLD_BUT_FEES_CONSUME_IT` (once `SETTLEMENT_DIFFERENCE_PRICED_VALUE_IS_ZERO`),
the ECONOMIC total never. The packet hour (row 7) and the packet-hour fixture
have no order-dependent SOFTWARE cell under any order of their decisions, which the test proves by
permutation (their only ties, with the fixture's `decided_at` unknown, are 9 events between ECONOMIC
codes at EV).
