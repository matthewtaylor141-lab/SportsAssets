## 4. Per-side markets: one book per side slug?

- What the venue nets: one signed position per SLUG (`position_side :53-81`). On a per-side market side A and side B are two slugs; a long of B on its slug is NOT netted against a long of A on A's slug — the account would hold both (the Halys+Kwon "guaranteed-loss shape" the one-per-game rule was written for, `le:8631-8638`).
- `_choose_long :217-246`: both tokens resolve BUY_LONG on different slugs -> his larger fills-derived side is the long, traded on ITS slug, `per_side: True` (`:232-237`; pinned tests/test_mirror_shadow.py:660-705, "whichever row is newer", "both long on ONE slug is ambiguous: refused"). `his_net = his_long - his_other` (ms:441) is then a number across two venue slugs: his_other reduces the target on slug A although nothing on slug A represents it.
- One book per side-slug with TWO standing rows is NOT sound under today's referees: (a) `_us_game_key` strips the side suffix — probe: `atc-eflch-bur-mid-2026-09-02-bur` and `...-mid` both -> `eflch-bur-mid-2026-09-02` — and the one-per-game read (`le:8645-8660`, widened `OR lane = 'mirror'`) refuses the second side of one game (`mirror_books_game_idx` 047:76 exists for the same claim; addendum section 7 "verify the sibling slug shares the game_key ... if not, admission refuses" — it DOES share it, so per P1's own rule a second book on the sibling slug is refused, and a long book on B while a long book on A stands would be two opposed positions, which the account cannot net). (b) 045 `live_orders_one_fill_per_asset` (`045:44-47`) keys on the TOKEN, so it would allow a B row beside an A row — the game key is the only guard.
- The sound shape is ONE book per game with a signed target in "side-A shares" where a negative target is expressed on the venue as... two different things: on aec- a negative target is BUY_SHORT on the same slug (one position, nets); on per-side it can be EITHER BUY_SHORT on A's slug (proven live: `SHORTROW atc-scp-dfc-joh-...-dfc ... ORDER_SIDE_SELL`, same call as aec-) OR BUY_LONG on B's slug. The first keeps one slug, one netted position, one standing row and needs no per-side special case at all — his BUY of B at q is our BUY_SHORT of A at long-price 1 - q (`sell_price(1-q, ask_A)`), the same equivalence as aec-. The only per-side differences: the BBO must be read on A's slug for both directions (`ms._paced_bbo` :365-368 already keys on the book's slug), and `_choose_long`'s "larger side is the long" must be replaced by a FIXED long side per game for the life of the book (else his side flipping re-picks the slug and the book's `long_asset` changes under it — today `_choose_long` re-runs every tick, ms:430). With that, `per_side_unsupported` can be lifted with `long_asset` pinned at open, and the never-add/one-per-game guards need nothing (one slug, one game). Hand-off (spec section 3) is whole-whale and already keys the mirror mode on username, not slug; `_mirror_owns_asset` (`le:7493`) reads `long_asset OR other_asset`, so his B-token fills are claimed by an A-slug book.
- Never-add guard note: per-side + BUY_SHORT of A is a position the desk's `account_holds` (`pmus:104`) does not see (netPosition < 0), so the per-fill lane's no-stack referee would not block a per-fill BUY of B on B's slug for another whale; the mirror's game claim (standing row, `OR lane='mirror'` `le:8657`) does.
